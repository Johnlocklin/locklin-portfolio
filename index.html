About Me

I'm John Locklin. I grew up studying creative design - Painting, graphics, illustration, design principles, color theory. You name it. But - when it came time to work, I chased the paycheck and left the creative side behind to work a corporate job. Now, 10 years later, I am done with the black and white life of corporate and ready to reintroduce some color into my life. Enter: Creative Software Dev - an opportunity to use my technical savvy alongside my creative prowess and home it into projects I can lose myself into. I hope you stay a while, and enjoy this space.
